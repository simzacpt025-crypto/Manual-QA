# Test Strategy

## Test Object

The Swag Labs login-to-checkout journey in Microsoft Edge.

## Test Basis

- **REQ-01:** A valid shopper can reach inventory.
- **REQ-02:** A selected product remains available in the cart.
- **REQ-03:** A shopper with complete checkout information can finish an order.

## Product Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Test Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Authentication failure | Medium | High | High |
| Lost cart contents | Medium | High | High |
| Incorrect validation | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| Failed order completion | Medium | High | High |
